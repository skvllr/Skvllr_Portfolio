// Hello!

I'm Skvllr but you can call me Muwder.

I'm a self-taught artist focused on digital illustration and frame-by-frame animation. I’m open to freelance commissions, personal projects and creative collaborations. This archive is a collection of my work, services and commission information.

I started with traditional sketches and gradually moved into digital art, character illustration and animation. I’m always learning, experimenting and developing my style.

// Work with me

Commission request

How commissions work

  1. Choose a service.
    Browse the price catalogue and decide what kind of artwork or animation you need.
  2. Contact me.
    Reach out through Discord or email. Include a short description of your idea and any visual references you have.
  3. Discuss the details.
    We will confirm the scope, character details, style, deadline, final price and any additional options.
  4. Payment & work process.
    Accepted payment methods: BLIK, bank transfer and PayPal. Payment details and the work schedule will be agreed privately before I begin.
  5. Progress & delivery.
    I will keep you updated. Once the work is complete and approved, you will receive the final files.
WILL DRAW
Original characters (OCs), fanart, furry, ships, soft gore, gore and NSFW.

WON'T DRAW
Mecha, vehicles, proship content and political content.

I reserve the right to decline a request. Please do not repost, edit, redistribute or claim my work without permission. Usage rights and any commercial use must be discussed before the commission begins.
